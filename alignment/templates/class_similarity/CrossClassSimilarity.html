How to use this page

This page shows cross-class relationships between GPCR classes.

  • Green (ID) = sequence identity (upper triangle).
  • Blue (SIM) = sequence similarity (lower triangle).
  • Hover any cell to preview pairs; click to lock and open detailed flyout.
  • Use the Download buttons to export the heatmap or the flyout as JPG/PNG/SVG.
  • “Human only” toggles scope; badges show the axis meaning.

You can tweak the scope, export with badges, and follow links to GPCRdb, GtoPdb, and UniProt.
