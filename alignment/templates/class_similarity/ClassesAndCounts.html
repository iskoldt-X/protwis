Each GPCR class also has receptor families based on shared endogenous ligand (classes A, B1, B2, C and F) or sequence similarity (classes D, E, OR, T2 and V). These are too many to list here but are included in the receptor classification information/selection across GPCRdb data and tools. Receptors and receptor families are named by the nomenclature committee of the International Union of Pharmacology and are available in their database Guide to Pharmacology.
